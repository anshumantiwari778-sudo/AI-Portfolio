01Profile

A mathematician building the proofs behind trust — and the mentor behind a new generation of AI students.

Prof. Namita Tiwari is Professor in the Department of Mathematics, School of Basic Sciences, and Dean (Research & Development) at Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University, Kanpur. She holds a Ph.D. in Cryptography from MNNIT Allahabad and an M.Sc. in Pure Mathematics from IIT Kanpur.

Her research spans pairing-free proxy signature families over elliptic curves, multivariate and lattice-based post-quantum candidates, and the security of biometric and blockchain systems — published in Elsevier, Springer, Wiley and IEEE venues, and protected across eleven patents.

Alongside her research, she founded and leads the AI & Data Science Forum, a student-facing initiative that has run 120+ lectures and workshops guiding 500+ students into applied AI, machine learning and data science.

02Research pillars

Four questions that shape the lab.

01

Elliptic Curve Cryptography

Pairing-free proxy, blind and group signature schemes built on ECDLP, with provable security in the random oracle model.

02

Digital Signatures

Proxy, multi-proxy, designated-verifier, fail-stop and collective signature families — design, cryptanalysis and repair.

03

Post-Quantum Cryptography

Multivariate systems (HFE, Matsumoto–Imai, Oil-Vinegar), NTRU lattices and hash-based schemes as NIST-track candidates.

04

AI, Biometrics & Security

Cancelable iris cryptosystems, ear-and-palmprint multimodal authentication, and drift-aware intrusion detection.
